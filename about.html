A Letter From Our Chairman

Dear Friend,

When my father, Harold Whitmore, started The American Family out of his garage in Council Bluffs, Iowa back in 1957, he had a simple idea: make good, honest products that bring families together. Forty-one years later, I'm proud to say we've never lost sight of that dream.

Today, we employ over 1,200 hard-working Americans in 4 states, and our products can be found in more than 3 million homes. And now we're on the World Wide Web! My grandson tells me this is going to be very big.

So on behalf of all of us here at The American Family, thank you for your business, and God bless.

Sincerely,
Dale R. Whitmore
Chairman & C.E.O.
