About Our Company

"Because Every Family Deserves to Feel Safe at Home."

A Letter From Our Chairman

Dear Friend,

When my father, Harold Whitmore, came home from the war, he had seen some things he didn't like to talk about. What he would talk about was this: every American family deserves to feel safe in their own home. So in 1957, he started The American Family out of his garage in Council Bluffs, Iowa, installing deadbolts for his neighbors.

Forty-one years later, we protect over 3 million homes in all 50 states (and parts of Canada) with security systems, reinforced doors, shelters and more. And now we're on the World Wide Web! My grandson tells me this is going to be very big.

So on behalf of all of us here at The American Family, thank you for trusting us with what matters most. Lock your doors, check your windows, and God bless.

Sincerely,
Dale R. Whitmore
Chairman & C.E.O.
